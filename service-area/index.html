Service Area: Forest Grove and Four Nearby Towns

Call (503) 974-3329

Our base is 2625 Pacific Ave in Forest Grove, ZIP 97116, and the phone line covers four nearby towns: Cornelius, Hillsboro, Banks and North Plains. Call (503) 974-3329 from any of them and a dispatcher will confirm your address and send a licensed plumber.

Distances below are from our Pacific Avenue address and are approximate. They are meant to help you see how the towns sit around Forest Grove, not to promise an arrival time. Roads, weather and call volume change how long any trip takes.

How Coverage Works

Coverage works town by town. If your address is in one of the five places above, the line serves it, around the clock. The dispatcher confirms the address on the phone, notes the nearest cross streets and sends a plumber who is licensed to do the work.

ZIP codes are a guide, not a rule. They are mail routes, and city limits often cross them. Cornelius is addressed mostly in 97113 but small pieces are in 97123 and 97124. Hillsboro uses both 97123 and 97124, and some of its eastern edge falls in 97006. Banks lists 97109 and 97125 as aliases for rural neighbors. Tell the dispatcher your street address, and the dispatcher will sort out the rest.

Water, sewer and permit rules change at the town line, too. A plumber working in Hillsboro deals with the Hillsboro Development Services and Permitting Center. In Cornelius, building services come from the City of Forest Grove Building Division. Banks and North Plains use Washington County for plumbing permits. Knowing those differences up front keeps jobs moving.

Unsure whether your address is covered? Give a dispatcher your street and town and get an answer.

Forest Grove

Forest Grove is the oldest city in Washington County, incorporated in 1872, with a 2020 Census population of 26,225. Housing here skews older than the rest of the area, with a median year built of 1982 and about 48 percent of homes built before 1980. Older homes tend to have older drain lines, interior piping and water heaters.

The City runs the water utility, and customers should never turn water off at the city meter. The City's Public Works line is 503-992-3258. The private sewer lateral is the owner's responsibility under City Ordinance 52.03, and the City's after-hours sewer emergency line is 503-412-2444.

In the historic neighborhoods south of downtown, including the Clark and Painter's Woods districts, houses were built between the late 1800s and the middle of the last century. Plumbers there can meet old cast iron, mixed pipe materials and walls that were never meant to be opened easily.

Cornelius and Hillsboro to the East

Cornelius is the closest neighbor, a short drive east along Baseline Street. It has a population of 12,694 and a median home year built of 1992, with a small older core near the railroad tracks. The City of Cornelius buys its water from Hillsboro, and owners are responsible from the meter to the house.

Hillsboro is the county seat and the largest city in the county, with 106,447 residents in 2020. Its housing is newer on average, with a median year built of 1997, though the downtown core and some older streets date earlier. Hillsboro issues its own plumbing permits, including for water heater replacement, and owners are responsible for their sewer laterals.

Banks and North Plains to the North

Banks sits about 6 miles north along Oregon Route 47 and is small, with 1,837 residents in 2020. The city draws on springs and wells, Clean Water Services provides sewer service, and some properties are on septic systems or private wells. Those carry different responsibilities, which is why a call from Banks starts with a question about how the property is served.

North Plains is about 7.5 miles northeast. It has grown quickly, from 1,947 people in 2010 to 3,441 in 2020, so many homes are newer. The city buys water from Hillsboro and the Joint Water Commission, and Washington County handles building and plumbing permitting. Gordon Road is known to flood in heavy rain between Commercial and Wascoe Streets.

If Your Address Is Outside These Towns

Call anyway. Some addresses sit just beyond a city limit or in unincorporated places like Verboort, Gales Creek or Helvetia, and the dispatcher can tell you honestly whether a plumber can reach you. The answer depends on the address and the situation at that moment.

If we cannot send someone, we will say so on the first call so you can keep looking. Waiting on a promise we cannot keep would be worse than a quick no. In an emergency, the immediate steps are the same anywhere. Close the main shutoff at your house, avoid the meter, and call 911 for gas.

You can read more about what plumbers handle on the plumbing services page, or reach us through the contact page for hours and the address.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you serve all of Washington County?

No. We serve Forest Grove, Cornelius, Hillsboro, Banks and North Plains, plus some nearby addresses. If you are outside those towns, call (503) 974-3329 and the dispatcher will tell you honestly whether a plumber can reach you.

Does my ZIP code decide whether I am covered?

Not alone. ZIP codes cross city lines, so we go by your street address and town. Cornelius includes 97113, 97123 and 97124. Hillsboro includes 97124, 97123 and part of 97006. Banks lists 97106, 97109 and 97125. Tell the dispatcher your address when you call.

Are distances an arrival time?

No. Distances are approximate, measured from 2625 Pacific Ave, and are there to show how the towns sit around Forest Grove. We do not promise an arrival time. Traffic, weather and call volume change every trip, and the dispatcher will be honest about what they know.
