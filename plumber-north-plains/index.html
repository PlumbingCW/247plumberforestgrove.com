Plumbing problems we see in North Plains homes

Homes built since 2010 tend to fail in the places you can see. A supply line under a sink loosens. A water heater tank leaks at a fitting or corrodes through. An outdoor faucet leaks after the first hard freeze because a hose was left on. A fitting behind a wall drips for months before a stain shows.

Older lots near the original townsite are a different story. Drain lines from earlier decades can be clay or cast iron, and tree roots find joints. We cannot name the pipe material by phone, so expect questions about when the house was built and whether it was ever repiped.

A new faucet is usually a quick job. Cost guides put a total faucet replacement at roughly $200 to $600. Anything inside a wall is different, and we explain why before opening it. For worn handles and drips, see our faucet repair page.

Water, sewer and permits in North Plains

The City of North Plains buys its water from the City of Hillsboro and the Joint Water Commission, which draws from the Tualatin River. Northwest Natural Water Services operates the system, and the city keeps an emergency backup well only. No lead service lines have been found. The water is not fluoridated.

The city's hydrant flushing can stir up rust in older pipes and turn water brown for a short time. Run a cold tap for a few minutes before assuming something is wrong inside the house.

Who owns what matters. If a leak is between the meter and your home, call a licensed plumber. From the meter to the street is Public Works. The sewer is separate. Clean Water Services provides sewer and treats wastewater at its Hillsboro plant, so residents hold accounts with both the city and CWS. The lateral from your house to the public sewer is yours.

A blocked lateral is a job for sewer line cleaning. Cost guides such as Angi report a main sewer clog averaging $380 with most jobs from $187 to $572.

Washington County handles building and plumbing permits in North Plains. A water heater replacement needs a permit, and the person doing the work pulls it. A faucet that is not concealed in a wall does not. Thermal expansion protection and relief valve routing are code items on a swap, and the heater must be strapped.

Backflow devices on irrigation systems and boilers must be tested when installed, once a year, and after any repair, by a qualified tester. Cost guides report about $30 to $300 per device.

Before We Arrive: What to Do at Home in North Plains

Close the main, not the meter

Turn off the main shutoff valve inside or at the house. If it will not close, tell the dispatcher. Do not wrestle with the meter box.

Kill power to a wet heater

If the water heater is leaking, switch off its breaker or gas control first. Smell gas? Get out and call NW Natural at 800-882-3377 or 911 from outside.

Open a low tap

Once the main is off, open the lowest cold tap to drain what is left in the pipes and slow the leak.

Note what you saw

Photograph the damage and write down when it started. It helps us, and it helps if you file a claim.

Winter and storm calls

Winters here are wet more than frozen. At the Forest Grove weather station, December averages 8.72 inches of precipitation and January 7.19. January lows average 32.6 F, so hard freezes come only now and then. When one does, pipes in garages, crawlspaces, attics and outside walls are the ones that split.

The city's advice is simple. Keep the thermostat at 55 F or higher, disconnect hoses before the first freeze, and thaw a frozen pipe with a hair dryer, heat lamp or space heater. Never use an open flame. Cost guides report thawing at a flat fee near $100 and a burst pipe repair averaging about $500 (Angi).

Heavy rain brings its own problem. Gordon Road floods between Commercial and Wascoe Streets, so do not assume a flooded stretch is passable. If you live near it, tell the dispatcher which way you can be reached.

  1. Shut the main and drain the lines if a pipe has split.
  2. Open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls to let in warm air.
  3. Keep a trickle running at the tap farthest from the meter.
  4. Call a licensed plumber for a burst pipe repair before the thaw makes it worse.

Sewage coming up in a tub or floor drain after a storm, or water spraying inside a wall, is an emergency. Stop all water use, shut the main, and call right away.

If you are out near Helvetia or Pumpkin Ridge Golf Club, tell us the nearest cross street. Hillsboro supplies North Plains with water but issues its own plumbing permits, so the paperwork differs. Banks runs a different water setup altogether.

Replacing a worn-out water heater

Storage water heaters last roughly 10 to 15 years. A tank leaking at the seam or hot water that runs out fast points toward a swap. Cost guides report standard tank installs at $600 to $3,100 (HomeGuide) or $882 to $1,826 (Angi). Our water heater replacement page covers sizing and permits.

Plan the swap on a weekday if you can. If the tank is flooding tonight, though, call (503) 974-3329 and say it is an emergency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is emergency service available in North Plains?

Yes. Our dispatch line is answered 24/7, day or night, including weekends and storms. A licensed plumber is sent from Forest Grove, about 7.5 miles away in a straight line. You hear a clear price before work starts. While you wait, shut the main valve at the house and keep clear of standing water.

Do you cover every ZIP code in North Plains?

North Plains has one ZIP code, 97133, and it is the only one for the city. Every North Plains address uses it, and we serve all of them. Some nearby rural addresses may carry other ZIPs, so if your property sits outside town, give the dispatcher your street address and cross road.

Who fixes a leak between the water meter and my North Plains house?

A leak between the meter and your home is a job for a licensed plumber like ours. The stretch from the meter to the street belongs to Public Works. If you are unsure where a leak sits, call us and we will trace it. Do not tamper with the meter box.

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater in North Plains?

Yes. Replacing a water heater needs a permit, and in North Plains Washington County handles plumbing permits. The person doing the work pulls it. Swapping a faucet that is not concealed in a wall does not need one. We will tell you what your job requires when you call.
