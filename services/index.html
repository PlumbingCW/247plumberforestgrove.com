What We Handle

Plumbing Services in Forest Grove, OR

Call (503) 974-3329

Plumbing is a big trade, and the same pipe can fail in many ways. This page sorts our eighteen services into five groups so you can find the one that matches what you see, hear or smell at home. If you already know what you need, the cards below go straight to each service page.

If you do not know, that is fine. Call (503) 974-3329 and describe the problem in your own words. A dispatcher can usually tell from a few questions which group it belongs to and whether it is an emergency or something to schedule.

Drains and Sewer: When Water Will Not Go Away

The first group covers anything where water leaves the house too slowly or not at all. A single slow sink usually means a clog in the trap or the branch line, and drain cleaning clears it. When several fixtures back up together, or a floor drain gurgles when the washing machine drains, the blockage is probably in the main line.

Heavier clogs from grease, roots or years of buildup need more than a snake. Hydro jetting uses a high-pressure water stream to scour the pipe wall, which the EPA describes as the most common hydraulic cleaning technique. For routine clearing of the main, sewer line cleaning is the usual starting point.

When cleaning does not hold, a camera looks inside. Sewer inspection shows cracks, offset joints, roots and sags. If the pipe itself has failed, sewer line repair covers spot fixes and replacement. In Forest Grove the private lateral from your house to the public sewer connection is the owner's responsibility, so these diagnoses matter.

Water Heaters: Hot Water, Installed Right

Four services deal with hot water. Water heater repair fits a heater that is cold, noisy or leaking at a fitting or valve. Repair is worth it when the tank is sound. A tank that leaks from its body is finished, and the question becomes replacement.

Water heater replacement swaps a failed unit for a new one of the same type. New water heater installation covers a first-time install or moving the heater, which means new gas, venting or water lines. Tankless water heaters heat on demand and last longer than tanks, but they cost more up front and have their own venting and gas needs.

A permit is needed to replace a water heater in Forest Grove, and heaters must be anchored or strapped under the Oregon Plumbing Specialty Code. Gas piping and flue work need a mechanical permit. Anyone who installs or repairs LP gas appliances or piping in Oregon must be licensed.

Fixtures: Toilets, Faucets and Disposals

The everyday repairs live here. Toilet repair handles running water, weak flushes, rocking bowls and leaks at the base. Most are small parts, such as a flapper, fill valve or wax ring. Dye tablets, which the City of Forest Grove hands out, help you find a silent flapper leak before the water bill does.

Faucet repair covers drips, loose handles and low flow, often a worn cartridge or clogged aerator. Garbage disposal repair deals with jams, hums and leaks under the sink. A jammed disposal often frees with a hex key, but one that leaks from its body usually needs replacing.

Replacing a sink, toilet or faucet that is not concealed in a wall does not need a permit in Oregon. That keeps these jobs simple, and most can be done in a single visit.

Leaks and Pipes: Finding and Fixing Water Loss

A leak you can see is a nuisance. A leak you cannot see is expensive. Leak detection finds the source with listening equipment, pressure testing and moisture checks, which avoids tearing out walls on a guess. The EPA says the average household leaks more than 9,300 gallons a year, so a water bill that creeps up deserves attention.

When a pipe fails outright, burst pipe repair is the emergency service. In freeze-prone spots like crawlspaces, attics and exterior walls, a split can happen after a cold night. For the line that brings water from the street to the house, water service line repair applies, and the City of Forest Grove Public Works can tell you where its responsibility ends.

Old, corroded or repeatedly failing pipes may call for whole-house repiping instead of patch repairs. That is a larger project that takes days in a smaller home and longer in a big one, with a clear price up front.

Commercial Work and New Installations

Businesses have plumbing needs of their own. Commercial plumbing covers restaurants, shops, offices and other small business spaces, where downtime costs money and fixtures see heavy use. Emergency calls for a backed-up restroom or a leaking supply line get the same dispatch as a home.

For remodels and new construction, plumbing installation covers moving fixtures, adding bathrooms, rough-in work and appliance hookups. Relocating plumbing, altering piping inside walls and building sewer or water service work need permits, and the person doing the work pulls them.

Prices and Permits on Any Service

Whatever the service, the order of events is the same. A licensed plumber looks at the problem, tells you what it is, offers options and gives a clear price before work starts. Public cost guides report ranges for many jobs, and each service page quotes the ones that apply, always as ranges from guides, not as our price.

Permits depend on the job. A plumber replacing a water heater or changing concealed piping pulls the permit. For work in Forest Grove, that means the Community Development office at 503-992-3229. Other towns have their own offices. The contact page has the hours, the address and what to do while you wait.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which service to ask for?

You do not have to. Call (503) 974-3329, describe what you see, hear or smell, and the dispatcher will match it to the right service. The plumber confirms the cause on site and explains the options with a clear price before work starts.

Do all eighteen services come with emergency response?

The line answers 24/7 for any plumbing problem. Some jobs, like a dripping faucet, are better scheduled, and the dispatcher will say so. Urgent ones such as burst pipes, sewage backups and leaking water heaters are treated as emergencies on the phone. We do not promise arrival times.

Do you work on gas lines?

Gas piping and flue work need a mechanical permit, and LP gas work needs a licensed installer. But if you smell gas, we do not diagnose it by phone. Leave the building and call NW Natural at 800-882-3377 or 911 from outside.
