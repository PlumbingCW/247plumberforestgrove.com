Burst Pipe Emergency: The First Steps When a Pipe Lets Go

  1. Close the main shutoff valve at the house, usually where the line enters a garage, crawlspace or utility room.
  2. Open the lowest faucet in the house and one upstairs tap to drain the line and drop the pressure.
  3. Switch off power to wet areas at the breaker only if you can reach it without standing in water.
  4. Move furniture, rugs and valuables out of the wet zone and photograph the damage.
  5. Call a licensed plumber.

The meter rule matters here. Forest Grove policy says customers and contractors never turn water off at the city meter. If someone closes it and the meter or line breaks, the repair is at the homeowner's expense. If your house valve is stuck, call Public Works at 503-992-3258 and the City will close it, with no fee in business hours and a fee after hours.

Why Pipes Split When the Weather Turns

The Red Cross explains it simply. Water expands as it freezes. A pipe full of ice has nowhere for the extra volume to go, and pressure builds until the pipe splits. The split is often not where the ice sits. Pressure trapped between an ice plug and a closed faucet is what finds the weak spot.

The thaw is when the leak shows up. A pipe that cracked overnight may be sealed by ice until midday, then begin to spray. NOAA's 1991 to 2020 normals put the Forest Grove January average low at 32.6 F and December at 32.8 F. Hard freezes here are occasional, which is why people are caught off guard. The risky spots are crawlspaces, attics, garages and outside walls.

Frozen Pipe, No Leak Yet

If a faucet gives a trickle or nothing in cold weather, leave it open and warm the pipe slowly. The Red Cross says to use a heating pad or hair dryer. Never use an open flame, blowtorch or propane heater, and the City of Cornelius gives the same warning. Work from the faucet end back toward the frozen section so melting water can escape.

If one pipe froze, others may have. Open cabinet doors under sinks, keep the thermostat at 55 F or above, and let cold water drip at the faucet farthest from the meter. If you cannot find the frozen section or the pipe is inside a wall, call (503) 974-3329 and a plumber can thaw it. Cost guides such as Angi and HomeAdvisor report a frozen pipe thaw at about $100.

A repair can be as small as cutting out a few inches and joining new pipe, or as large as replacing a run through the whole wall. If the split came from age and not cold, other sections may be close behind. That is when a plumber may suggest repiping instead of repeated patches.

Permits When the Break Is Inside a Wall

Under Oregon Building Codes Division rules, emergency repair of freeze-damaged or leaking concealed piping needs a permit if the new piping exceeds five feet. The person doing the work, a homeowner or a contractor, pulls it. In Forest Grove that is the City Community Development office at 1924 Council St, 503-992-3229. A permit lapses if work has not started in 180 days.

A short patch inside a wall under five feet falls outside that rule. Plumbers know where the line is and will tell you which side of it your repair falls on. An unpermitted run behind drywall can cause trouble later when you sell, so ask about it.

Water Damage, Insurance and What to Document

Sudden pipe bursts are the kind of loss homeowner policies often cover, though what they pay for varies. Many do not cover the pipe itself. Call your insurer early and ask what they need. If the break took out a fixture, a faucet repair or a water heater repair often follows once the pipe is sound.

  • Photos and video of the damage before cleanup starts.
  • The plumber's written description of what failed and what was repaired.
  • Receipts for fans, dehumidifiers and emergency drying.
  • The date and time the leak started, as closely as you can tell.

Drying matters as much as the pipe. Wet drywall and insulation grow mold quickly in damp weather, and Forest Grove gets 46.72 inches of precipitation a year. Open windows when the outside air is dry enough to help, and run fans.

Stopping the Next Burst Before Winter Does It Again

Prevention is cheap. Disconnect hoses before the first freeze, close the inside valves feeding outdoor bibbs, and drain them. Insulate pipes in crawlspaces and attics. Let a faucet drip when a hard freeze is forecast, and keep the heat on, even in rooms you do not use.

Housing age raises the stakes. About 48 percent of Forest Grove homes were built before 1980, and 1,030 date to 1939 or earlier, often with pipes in cold crawlspaces and exterior walls. If the break is between the meter and the house, see water service line repair. Persistent small drips deserve leak detection before they become the next burst.

The same freeze problem shows up in Hillsboro, where the city notes that repairs on the customer's side of the meter are the owner's responsibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle burst pipe repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Forest Grove?

Yes. Dispatch runs 24/7, including nights and weekends. Call (503) 974-3329 and a licensed plumber is sent, with a clear price given before work starts. We make no arrival-time promise, so close the main valve at the house, open the lowest faucet and move belongings out of the wet area while you wait.

Can I shut off water at the street meter in Forest Grove?

No. City policy is that customers and contractors never turn the water off at the meter. If the meter or line breaks as a result, repairs are at the homeowner's expense. Close the main valve at the house instead. If it fails, call Public Works at 503-992-3258 and the City will shut it off.

Is it safe to thaw a frozen pipe myself?

Yes if you use a hair dryer or heating pad, with the faucet open. Never use a torch, open flame or propane heater. If you cannot reach the frozen section, see a crack or wet spot, or smell something burning, stop and call a licensed plumber. Remember that a second pipe may be frozen too.

Do I need a permit to repair a burst pipe?

Emergency repair of freeze-damaged or leaking concealed piping needs a permit when the new piping exceeds five feet. Exposed pipe repairs generally do not. The City of Forest Grove Community Development office at 503-992-3229 issues plumbing permits and can confirm your situation.

Will insurance cover my burst pipe?

Many homeowner policies cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe but not the pipe itself or slow seepage. Coverage varies, so call your insurer early. Take photos before cleanup and keep the plumber's invoice and description of the failure to support your claim.
